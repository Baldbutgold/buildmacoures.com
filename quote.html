Before you ask

Common questions

Is the blueprint really free?

Yes, no strings attached. It is how we show the standard of our thinking before you spend anything, and it is yours to keep even if you build the course with someone else.

What does a build actually cost?

Builds start at $3,000 and are quoted as one fixed price for the whole program. No hourly billing, and you see the full number in writing before anything starts. Larger multi-module programs run higher, and the blueprint tells you exactly where yours lands.

How do I know the quality is any good?

If the blueprint and quote work for you, we produce one finished lesson from your own material, free, before you sign anything. Script, slides, voiceover, rendered video. You judge the real thing, not a promise.

Will it work in our LMS, and who owns it?

We deliver SCORM-ready packages that drop into most platforms, and can build directly in Moodle or Google Classroom. You get full IP and the editable source files on delivery.

What if our material is a mess, or barely written?

That is normal and it is fine. Most of what we receive is a mix of slides, SOPs, and things that only exist in someone's head. Tell us that on the form and the blueprint will account for the gap.

How long does it take?

A single course is usually 2 to 4 weeks from approved scope. Multi-module programs run longer, and the timeline is laid out in your blueprint so there are no surprises.
